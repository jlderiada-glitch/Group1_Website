North Negros College

North Negros College (NNC) is a private higher education institution in Cadiz City, Negros Occidental, committed to delivering quality and values-based education.

Guided by the philosophy “Education is our passion,” NNC empowers students through academic excellence, skills development, and community involvement.

With diverse programs in Senior High School, College, and TESDA certified assessment centers, NNC continues to shape future-ready Nornecans who lead with purpose and serve with heart.

Bachelor of Science in Computer Science

The Bachelor of Science in Computer Science program develops students' knowledge and skills in computing, programming, software development, databases, networking, data, algorithms, and emerging technologies.

Students are encouraged to become creative problem-solvers who can design technological solutions for real-world challenges.
